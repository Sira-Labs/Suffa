# Pilot kick-off interview (story 4.5)

A 45-minute conversation with the teacher before the class is set up (pilot plan step 1 in
`docs/plan/engagement-plan.md` §7). The product owner leads; answers go into the three
records at the end of this page. Questions are written the way they can be asked.

## Before the call

- Teacher account ready: the teacher signs in once at the app; an admin sets the role to
  _Lehrkraft_ under Verwaltung (the change is audit-logged).
- Have a phone at hand to show the join flow: class → Einladungslink & QR-Code → scan.

## 1. The class (10 min)

- How many learners, and how many of them are under 18? Under 16?
- Which levels – all starting at unit 1 of book 1, or mixed?
- Which devices do they use: own phones (Android / iPhone), shared tablets, school laptops?
  Do all have an email address they can open on the same device?
- Languages at home besides German (for explanations and support)?

## 2. Rhythm (10 min)

- When do you meet (days, times, length)? Online, in person or both?
- How much should learners practise between sessions – minutes per day, days per week?
- Which units do you plan for the pilot period (January–February)?
- How do you check progress today (tests, homework, nothing formal)?

## 3. Recordings and media (10 min)

- Do you record sessions? Where are the recordings (Google Drive, phone, none)?
- Who appears in them – only you, or learners too?
- Which recordings should learners see, and for how long?

## 4. Consent and data (10 min)

- For minors: who gives consent – parents in writing, the school, the association?
- Is there a data-protection contact at your institution we should inform?
- What may you as teacher see? Suffa shows teachers only their own classes; learners see
  their classes, never each other's data. Leaderboards are opt-in (name + score only).
- Explain export and deletion: every learner can download all their data and delete their
  account under Einstellungen → Meine Daten.

## 5. Wrap-up (5 min)

- Walk through the join flow once on the teacher's phone.
- Agree the date for class setup with 3–5 test learners (pilot plan step 2, end of S4).
- Agree how the teacher reaches us during the pilot (channel, response time).
